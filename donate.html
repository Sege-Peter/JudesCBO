Where your gift goes

Your Support Powers Four Programs

Ending SGBV

Referrals to health, legal aid, psychosocial support and safe houses for survivors.

Youth Health

Sexuality education, contraceptives, HIV testing and youth-friendly services.

Mental Health

Peer support groups, counseling and safe spaces for young people.

Disability Inclusion

Inclusive education, family support and skills for children with Cerebral Palsy.

Registered since 2018

A community-based organization registered in Homa Bay County.

Trusted partners

Working with the Ministry of Health, Homa Bay County, Tiko Africa and the Dutch Flower Foundation.

Integrity first

Questions

Donation FAQs

How do I know my M-Pesa donation reached you?

Before entering your PIN, M-Pesa shows the name of the recipient. After sending, message us your M-Pesa confirmation code on WhatsApp or by email and we'll confirm we received it.

Can I give to a specific program?

Yes. Choose a cause above before tapping "I've donated, let us know" and your message will tell us which program to put your gift towards.

Can I donate items instead of money?

Absolutely. Items like sanitary pads, school supplies and mobility aids make a real difference. Contact us on WhatsApp to arrange a drop-off in Oyugis.

I'm outside Kenya. How can I give?

Email info@judescbo.org and we'll share the best way to send your donation from abroad, including bank transfer details.

Can my organisation partner with Judes CBO?

We'd love to hear from you. Visit our contact page or call +254 712 109310 to discuss a partnership.

We're happy to help you choose the best way to support our work.
