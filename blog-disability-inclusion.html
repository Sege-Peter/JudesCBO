Breaking Barriers: Disability Inclusion

March 12, 2023 · Judes CBO

Judes CBO is committed to supporting neurodiverse individuals and persons with Cerebral Palsy through inclusive education, vocational training, and community awareness programs.

Our focus

Our focus is on removing stigma, promoting dignity, and ensuring that every person, regardless of ability, can actively contribute to society and access equal opportunities.

By breaking barriers, we are paving the way for a more inclusive and compassionate community.
