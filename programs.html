What we do

Programs that protect, empower and uplift

We focus on four core areas that address the most pressing social challenges facing women, youth and persons with disabilities in Homa Bay County.

Program 01

End Sexual & Gender-Based Violence (SGBV)

Fighting SGBV, restoring dignity.

We stand with young women and youth in the fight against sexual and gender-based violence, strengthening referral pathways so survivors are supported, not silenced. Our holistic response restores dignity and builds safer communities.

  • Stronger referral pathways for survivors
  • Access to health services
  • Legal aid
  • Psychosocial support
  • Safe houses
  • Community reintegration
Need help now? Call the free national GBV helpline 1195, any time.

Program 02

Adolescent & Youth Sexual and Reproductive Health (AYSRH)

Informed choices, healthy futures.

We champion young people's right to make informed choices about their health and bodies, and work with schools and communities to break the stigma around reproductive health.

  • Comprehensive sexuality education
  • Contraceptive awareness
  • HIV/AIDS prevention
  • Maternal health
  • School and community outreach
  • Youth-friendly services
With Tiko Africa (Triggerise), we work across 10 civic wards in Homa Bay County to provide contraceptives, HIV testing and mental health support.

Mental Health & Psychosocial Support

Safe spaces to talk, heal and thrive.

We address mental health challenges such as depression, anxiety and drug abuse, building resilience and empowering youth to lead healthy, balanced lives.

  • Community outreach
  • Peer support groups
  • Counseling
  • Support for depression and anxiety
  • Drug abuse awareness
  • Resilience building

Program 04

Disability Inclusion

Dignity, acceptance and opportunity for all.

We promote inclusion for people with disabilities, with a special focus on neurodiversity and children living with Cerebral Palsy, so that everyone can participate fully and equally in society.

  • Inclusive education
  • Family support
  • Awareness campaigns
  • Skills development
  • Vocational training
  • Stigma reduction
