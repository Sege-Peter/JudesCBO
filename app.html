How to install

Three quick steps on any device

Android phone

In Chrome

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. Judes CBO appears with your apps.

iPhone or iPad

In Safari

  1. Tap the Share button .
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Judes CBO appears on your home screen.

Computer

In Chrome or Edge

  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser menu and choose Install Judes CBO.
  3. Click Install. It opens in its own window.
