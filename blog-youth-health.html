Youth and Adolescent Health Programme

August 20, 2023 · Judes CBO

At Judes CBO, we are committed to creating a future where young people can make informed choices about their health, education, and wellbeing. Our Youth and Adolescent Health Programme focuses on Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR) and mental health, with a strong emphasis on empowering adolescent girls and young women (AGYW).

What the programme offers

  • Comprehensive sexuality education tailored for adolescents and youth.
  • Access to youth-friendly reproductive health services in partnership with local facilities.
  • Mentorship and peer-support networks to guide young people in making better life choices.
  • Mental health awareness and counseling support for resilience and wellbeing.

Working with Tiko Africa

We are currently partnering with Tiko Africa (Triggerise) in Homa Bay County, working across 10 civic wards to provide contraceptives, HIV testing, and mental health support.

Together, we are building a healthier, brighter future where no girl is left behind because of unplanned pregnancies or lack of support.
